Noisy conveyor belt motor

Workplace task · 15 minutes

Scenario

The motor of a conveyor belt in the company is making a strange noise.

What to do

  1. Ask your usual AI tool: "What can cause a strange noise in a conveyor belt motor?" Write the causes it gives in your notes.
  2. Look up each cause in the troubleshooting page of the manual. Write what the manual says.
  3. Show your notes to your mentor. Write down what the mentor says about each cause.
  4. With your mentor, mark which cause you will check first.
Reward: complete all the checks to earn the Double-checker badge. It rewards checking, not whether the AI was right.
Approve and add to Phase 2
